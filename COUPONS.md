# mygpt.work Coupons: Four Chances, One Final Choice

[简体中文](./COUPONS.zh-CN.md)

The coupon experience is one of the clearest differences between mygpt.work and a basic one-code promotion. It gives the customer several transparent, user-initiated chances while enforcing one final discount per batch.

## How it works

1. The customer selects a plan and submits a coupon request email.
2. After approval, the system creates a batch of up to four candidates.
3. One email delivers the first coupon and the entry to the draw page.
4. During the next 30 minutes, the customer may actively draw three more times.
5. The same email entry can reopen the draw page during the activity window, preserving revealed results.
6. Applying any candidate immediately locks that coupon and ends the batch.
7. Order submission validates the one-coupon rule again and removes the other unused candidates.

## Rules that matter

- First coupon plus three optional draws equals up to four candidates.
- A result may be a fixed CNY amount or a percentage discount.
- Only one coupon from the batch can be used, and candidates cannot be stacked.
- One email can retain one unused draw batch per plan.
- The applied coupon is bound to the order email and applicable plan; each coupon is limited to one order.
- Coupon codes are credentials and should not be shared.

## Why customers benefit

- **Choice:** the first result is not the only possible result.
- **Transparency:** draw count, time window, and the one-coupon rule are explained in advance.
- **Recoverability:** the original email can reopen the activity during its valid period.
- **Conflict prevention:** application locks the coupon, and order creation validates the batch again.
- **Clear plan scope:** fixed-amount coupons use the plan shown with the coupon.
