# One Clear Subscription Flow

mygpt.work is designed to remove several common points of friction from a ChatGPT subscription in China.

## 1. Compare four plans in CNY

Go, Plus, Pro 5x, and Pro 20x appear together with their current CNY prices. Customers can choose by workload and request a coupon for the intended plan.

## 2. Create an order for the customer's own account

The login session is encrypted in the browser and used to verify the account for this order. mygpt.work does not substitute a shared account.

## 3. Lock the amount and pay locally

The order locks its payable amount. Alipay and WeChat are supported, so an overseas credit card is not required.

## 4. Let the system process the subscription

After payment confirmation, the system automatically performs account checks and subscription processing. The typical completion time is 5–10 minutes, with a maximum of 1 hour under normal service expectations.

## 5. Keep seeing what happens

The order page separates payment from fulfillment. The original browser has direct access, and email verification restores read-only visibility from another browser.

This combination of local payment, personal-account use, price locking, coupon choice, and trackable progress is the central mygpt.work advantage.
