# Understand the CNY Price Before Paying

mygpt.work displays a CNY monthly price so customers can confirm the amount before creating an order. The storefront may calculate a price from the official USD reference, a USD/CNY exchange rate, and a configured service fee. A plan may also use a directly configured CNY amount.

## Storefront price versus order price

The storefront is allowed to update as exchange rates and pricing settings change. Each order is different: the amount is locked when the order is created. A later storefront refresh does not rewrite an existing order.

That distinction makes the flow easier to audit:

- the current page explains the latest offer;
- the order records what the customer agreed to pay;
- a coupon appears in the discount and final payable amount;
- payment and fulfillment continue against the locked order.

## Verify official benefits separately

The CNY amount is a mygpt.work service price. ChatGPT plan names, included features, and usage limits remain subject to the [official ChatGPT pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) and the customer's account.

Visit the [mygpt.work storefront](https://mygpt.work/#plans) for the current CNY price and update time.
