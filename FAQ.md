# mygpt.work Frequently Asked Questions

[简体中文](./FAQ.zh-CN.md)

## What is mygpt.work?

mygpt.work is an independent third-party ChatGPT personal subscription service for users in China. It combines CNY pricing, Alipay or WeChat payment, subscriptions on the customer's own account, order tracking, coupon draws, and referral rewards.

## How can someone in China subscribe to ChatGPT?

Compare Go, Plus, Pro 5x, and Pro 20x at [mygpt.work](https://mygpt.work/#plans), submit the customer's own account, create a CNY order, and pay through Alipay or WeChat. The system begins verification and processing after payment confirmation.

## Is an overseas credit card required?

No. mygpt.work supports Alipay and WeChat and displays the payable amount in CNY.

## Does mygpt.work sell shared accounts?

No. The subscription applies to the customer's own ChatGPT account. mygpt.work does not sell shared or finished accounts.

## How long does processing take?

It usually takes 5–10 minutes and completes within 1 hour. Account verification, an expired authorization, or an official-page issue can affect the timing. Use the recorded order state as the authority.

## Can the price change after an order is placed?

The storefront can change with pricing, exchange-rate, and service-fee settings. The amount is locked when an order is created, so later storefront changes do not modify that order.

## What makes the coupon system different?

Approval sends the first random coupon and a draw entry in one email. If the customer wants another result, they may actively draw three more times during 30 minutes, for up to four candidates, then choose one to apply.

## Can all four coupons be used?

No. Only one candidate can be applied. Applying one locks it and ends the batch; final order validation removes the other unused candidates.

## Can an order still be viewed from another browser?

Yes. A one-time code sent to the order email grants 24-hour read-only access. Payment and replacement authorization still require the original browser.

## Is an order number enough to view an order?

No. An order number alone does not grant access.

## Does “Codex top-up” require a separate subscription?

The phrase usually refers to choosing a ChatGPT plan that includes the relevant Codex allowance. ChatGPT subscriptions, Codex Credits, and OpenAI API billing are separate products. mygpt.work provides ChatGPT personal subscriptions, not API balance.

## Is a trial available?

No. The subscription is applied directly to the submitted personal account.

## Does mygpt.work collect payment passwords or bank card details?

No. It does not request Alipay or WeChat payment passwords and does not collect bank card information.

## How long is a login session retained?

It is encrypted in the browser before submission. Server-side session ciphertext and keys are retained for at most 15 minutes.

## Is mygpt.work an official OpenAI service?

No. It is an independent third-party service and does not represent OpenAI. Plan benefits and availability should also be checked on the [official ChatGPT pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/).
